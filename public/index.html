Our philosophy

When intelligence is always on, a team should be able to answer two questions about an agent’s work. What did we ask it to do? What did we give it access to?

These questions shape what we build. With Cadre, a reviewer can trace a change back to an approved spec. With MCP Gateway, a developer can see which tools the team permits and choose which to enable.

Our products

Cadre

A spec-driven AI harness that turns an approved specification into a dependency-aware plan. Teams can review changes against the spec and resume interrupted work.
